## Outsourcing & contracts

**Q1. Opening your own development centre in Vietnam is an example of: (a) Domestic outsourcing (b) Captive offshoring (c) Insourcing (d) Nearshore outsourcing**
**Answer: (b) Captive offshoring** — the work moves abroad but stays in-house.

**Q2. Hiring another Indian IT company to do your software work is: (a) Offshoring (b) Reshoring (c) Domestic outsourcing (d) Vertical integration**
**Answer: (c) Domestic outsourcing** — third party, same country.

**Q3. In ABC Electronics' vendor problem, the weighted score of Vendor B is: (a) 7.75 (b) 8.10 (c) 8.20 (d) 7.50**
**Answer: (c) 8.20** — 270 + 200 + 140 + 120 + 90 = 820 ÷ 100.

**Q4. A logistics provider that manages other 3PLs and the whole supply chain for a client is a: (a) 1PL (b) 2PL (c) 3PL (d) 4PL**
**Answer: (d) 4PL** — the lead logistics provider/integrator.

**Q5. Hidden costs of $720,000 on an $8 million contract are what % of total outsourcing cost? (a) 9.00% (b) 8.26% (c) 7.20% (d) 10.0%**
**Answer: (b) 8.26%** — 720,000 ÷ 8,720,000.

**Q6. Risk score in a risk matrix is: (a) Probability + Impact (b) Probability × Impact (c) Impact ÷ Probability (d) Probability²**
**Answer: (b) Probability × Impact**

**Q7. A pre-agreed sum payable for delay, without proving actual loss, is: (a) Force majeure (b) Indemnity (c) Liquidated damages (d) Step-in right**
**Answer: (c) Liquidated damages**

**Q8. BATNA stands for: (a) Best Agreement To Negotiate Again (b) Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement (c) Basic Agreement on Terms and Negotiated Amounts (d) Buyer's Alternative To New Agreements**
**Answer: (b) Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement** — Fisher & Ury.

**Q9. Which sourcing strategy best supports resilience after an earthquake? (a) Single sourcing (b) Multi-sourcing (c) Sole sourcing (d) Spot buying only**
**Answer: (b) Multi-sourcing**

**Q10. In Que 13, order accuracy (24,100 of 24,500) is: (a) 98.37% (b) 98.50% (c) 99.00% (d) 97.37%**
**Answer: (a) 98.37%**

**Q11. In Que 13, which KPI meets its target? (a) Order accuracy (b) Inventory accuracy (c) Damage rate (d) Complaint rate**
**Answer: (d) Complaint rate** — 0.18% ≤ 0.2%.

**Q12. The 6Ms of a fishbone diagram do NOT include: (a) Man (b) Machine (c) Money market (d) Measurement**
**Answer: (c) Money market** — Man, Machine, Method, Material, Measurement, Mother nature.

**Q13. In Que 12 the first-year outsourcing cost is: (a) $11.0 m (b) $11.4 m (c) $12.0 m (d) $14.3 m**
**Answer: (c) $12.0 m** — 11 + 0.6 + 0.4.

**Q14. Total landed cost per unit from India (unit $145, tariff 5%) is: (a) $145.00 (b) $150.00 (c) $152.25 (d) $160.00**
**Answer: (c) $152.25**

**Q15. A clause letting the buyer inspect the supplier's factory and records is: (a) Audit rights (b) Force majeure (c) Change control (d) Liquidated damages**
**Answer: (a) Audit rights**

## Incoterms

**Q16. Incoterms are published by: (a) WTO (b) ICC (c) IMO (d) UNCTAD**
**Answer: (b) ICC** — International Chamber of Commerce, Paris.

**Q17. Incoterms were first published in: (a) 1919 (b) 1936 (c) 1953 (d) 1980**
**Answer: (b) 1936**

**Q18. How many Incoterms 2020 rules are there? (a) 9 (b) 11 (c) 13 (d) 7**
**Answer: (b) 11** — 7 any-mode + 4 sea.

**Q19. Which is a sea and inland waterway rule only? (a) FCA (b) CPT (c) FAS (d) DAP**
**Answer: (c) FAS** — with FOB, CFR, CIF.

**Q20. The only Incoterm requiring the seller to unload at destination is: (a) DAP (b) DDP (c) DPU (d) CPT**
**Answer: (c) DPU**

**Q21. DPU replaced which 2010 term? (a) DDU (b) DAT (c) DES (d) DAF**
**Answer: (b) DAT** — Delivered at Terminal.

**Q22. Under CIP (2020), the seller must insure for at least: (a) ICC (C), 100% (b) ICC (A), 110% (c) ICC (B), 110% (d) No insurance**
**Answer: (b) ICC (A), 110%**

**Q23. Under CIF, minimum insurance is: (a) ICC (A) (b) ICC (B) (c) ICC (C) (d) War risk only**
**Answer: (c) ICC (C)**

**Q24. Under which term does the buyer clear export? (a) FCA (b) EXW (c) FOB (d) CPT**
**Answer: (b) EXW**

**Q25. Under which term does the seller pay import duty? (a) DAP (b) DPU (c) CIF (d) DDP**
**Answer: (d) DDP**

**Q26. In CFR, risk passes: (a) At the destination port (b) On board at the port of shipment (c) At the seller's premises (d) After unloading**
**Answer: (b) On board at the port of shipment**

**Q27. The best Incoterm for containerised freight according to the PPT: (a) FOB (b) FCA (c) EXW (d) CFR**
**Answer: (b) FCA** — risk passes on delivery to carrier.

**Q28. Best Incoterm for high-value goods per the PPT: (a) CPT (b) CIP (c) EXW (d) FAS**
**Answer: (b) CIP** — comprehensive insurance by seller.

**Q29. Vaccines delivered to Berlin DC, buyer clears import: (a) DDP (b) DAP (c) CIF (d) FCA**
**Answer: (b) DAP**

**Q30. Buyer's forwarder collects machinery at Pune warehouse: (a) EXW (b) FCA (c) FOB (d) CPT**
**Answer: (b) FCA**

**Q31. Grapes to Jebel Ali, seller pays ocean freight, no insurance: (a) CIF (b) CFR (c) FOB (d) CPT**
**Answer: (b) CFR**

**Q32. Garments to Madrid Inland Terminal, multimodal, buyer insures: (a) CIP (b) CPT (c) CFR (d) DAP**
**Answer: (b) CPT**

**Q33. Incoterms do NOT determine: (a) Risk transfer (b) Cost allocation (c) Transfer of title (d) Export clearance responsibility**
**Answer: (c) Transfer of title**

**Q34. The most commonly used Incoterms for air freight are: (a) FOB, CFR, CIF (b) FCA, CPT, CIP (c) FAS, FOB, DDP (d) EXW, DAP, DPU**
**Answer: (b) FCA, CPT, CIP** — risk at the airport cargo terminal.

**Q35. Which term places maximum obligation on the seller? (a) EXW (b) DDP (c) CIF (d) DPU**
**Answer: (b) DDP**

**Q36. Correct way to write an Incoterm: (a) FOB (b) FOB China (c) FCA Warehouse 3, Shanghai, Incoterms® 2020 (d) Incoterms FCA**
**Answer: (c) FCA Warehouse 3, Shanghai, Incoterms® 2020**

**Q49. Free storage at port before charges (PPT) is about: (a) 1 day (b) 5 days (c) 14 days (d) 30 days**
